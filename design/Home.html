Our Story

A School Unlike
Any Other

Sharda Public School stands in the foothills of Almora, Uttarakhand — at the edge of the Kumaon Himalaya, where ancient Deodar forests meet some of the world's most spectacular mountain views.

We offer a complete education from Kindergarten through Class XII under the CBSE curriculum, supported by a faculty of dedicated educators drawn from across India and the world. Our students arrive from dozens of states; they leave as scholars, athletes, artists, and above all, thoughtful human beings.

Many of our alumni have gone on to the IITs and the nation's most prestigious universities — carrying the spirit of the mountains into every endeavour.

Principal's Message
"
Education at its finest is not merely the transfer of knowledge — it is the awakening of a young person's full potential.

At Sharda Public School, we believe that the greatest classroom in the world surrounds our campus — the ancient Deodar forests, the silence of the high peaks, the clarity of the Kumaon sky. These are not distractions from learning; they are learning itself.

Our faculty, drawn from across the world, come here not for comfort but for purpose. And our students — curious, resilient, extraordinary young people — rise to meet that purpose every single day. The results speak for themselves: in board marks, in IIT ranks, and above all in the kind of people our graduates become.

— The Principal, Sharda Public School
