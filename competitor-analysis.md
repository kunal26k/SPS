# School Website Competitor Analysis
## For: Sharda Public School Revamp — 2026

---

## Top School Websites Analyzed

### 1. Khan Lab School (Mountain View, CA)
- **Design:** Minimalist, full-screen immersive homepage video
- **Color:** Clean whites, purposeful accent color
- **Navigation:** Simple, minimal — student-first philosophy
- **CTAs:** Implicit enrollment focus
- **Standout:** Balances visual appeal with accessible navigation
- **Adopt for SPS:** Full-screen video/photo hero, clean minimal nav

---

### 2. The Walker School (Georgia, USA)
- **Design:** Bold rich media, horizontal scrolling narrative sections
- **Color:** Dark rich tones with bold accents
- **Navigation:** Clear hierarchy, admissions within 1-2 clicks
- **Standout:** Showcases 36,000+ sq ft science building through visual storytelling
- **Adopt for SPS:** Use STEM/AI lab as a hero differentiator, visual narrative

---

### 3. Ross School (East Hampton, NY)
- **Design:** Minimalist, high-impact aerial campus video, scroll animations
- **Color:** Warm palette, dark overlays
- **Navigation:** Separate category menus for divisions
- **CTAs:** Donation form embedded on homepage
- **Standout:** "Radically minimal" paired with live social media feed
- **Adopt for SPS:** Minimal clutter, aerial/wide campus shots, campus division navigation

---

### 4. Bishop Foley Catholic High School

- **Navigation:** Organized dropdown menus
- **Unique:** TikTok-style short-form student video content
- **CTAs:** "Apply Now," "Schedule a Visit"

### 5. Gilman School (Maryland, USA)

- **Navigation:** Organized dropdown

## Design Patterns to Adopt for SPS

| Pattern | Source Schools | Implementation |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| Full-screen video/photo hero | Khan Lab, Ross School, Walker School | Campus video loop or high-res photo with overlay |
| Stats/numbers bar | Multiple | Dark navy band — 1300+ Students · 50+ Faculty · 23 Years |
| Alumni college placement showcase | Gilman School | IIT Madras · Edinburgh · IIT Jodhpur · LSR Delhi on homepage |
| Audience-segmented quick access | Poudre School | "For Parents / For Students / Alumni" shortcuts |
| Student testimonial videos | Vista Charter, Bishop Foley | Video testimonials + parent quotes |
| Grade-level content segmentation | Summit Country Day | Program cards: Primary · Middle · Secondary · Sr. Secondary |
| Two-campus unified branding | Bright Star Schools | Junior & Senior branch with distinct but branded cards |
| Scroll-triggered animations | Ocean School, Paris Academy | Framer Motion reveal on stats, sections |
| Heritage/motto visual treatment | Zaytuna College | "Seva Parmo Dharma" as a visual anchor section |
| STEM/Tech lab as differentiator | Walker School | AI & Robotics Lab prominently featured — major selling point |
| WhatsApp instant contact | India market standard | Floating WhatsApp button throughout |
| Transparent → solid sticky nav | Multiple | Navbar transition on scroll |
| Social feed integration | Gilman, Ross School | Instagram feed embedded on homepage or Life at SPS page |

## What Top Sites Avoid (SPS Should Avoid Too)

| Anti-Pattern | Current SPS Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Generic quotes as hero | Socrates quote on hero | Replace with school's own brand voice |
| Placeholder content | Lorem ipsum on Vision page | Write actual vision statement |
| Vague statistics | "100 Collaborations" | Remove or explain clearly |
| Org-chart navigation | Navigation mirrors internal structure | Redesign around visitor intent |
| No audience segmentation | One path for everyone | Add parent / student / alumni pathways |
| No student voice | No testimonials/videos | Add parent/student quotes and video |
| Buried achievements | CBSE results with no specifics | Feature topper names/ranks prominently |
| Missing stream info | Class 11-12 streams unknown | Publish Science/Commerce/Arts clearly |

## Competitor Insights — Visual/Color Benchmarks

| School Type | Typical Colors | SPS Alignment |
|---|---|---|
| Premium/Elite Private | Dark navy + gold + white | Strong fit — SPS logo is already navy + gold |
| Modern progressive | Bright accent + white dominant | Use gold as accent on white base |
| Heritage/traditional | Deep blue/maroon + cream | Navy primary works well |
| Indian private schools | Saffron/green patriotic OR blue professional | Stick with brand — navy/green/gold is distinctive |

**SPS Brand Colors (from logo):**
- `#1A2E4A` — Navy (primary)
- `#2D6A3F` — Green (secondary)
- `#E8A820` — Gold (accent / CTA)
- `#FFFFFF` — White (dominant background)
- `#F7F8FA` — Light gray (section alternates)

## Summary: Key Differentiators SPS Should Highlight

1. **Robotics & AI Lab** — Very few Almora schools have this
2. **Swimming Pool** — Premium facility not common in the region  
3. **IIT & International Alumni** — IIT Madras, Edinburgh, Australia, IIT Jodhpur = credibility proof
4. **23 Years Legacy** — Trust and stability
5. **Two Campuses** — Dedicated junior and senior environments
6. **CBSE NCF 2024 + NEP 2020** — Modern curriculum alignment
7. **1:20 Teacher-Student Ratio** — Personal attention
8. **15 Sports Programs** — Holistic development signal

These 8 points should be front and center — visible within the first scroll on the homepage.
