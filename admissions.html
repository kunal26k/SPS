The Assessment · Step 3 of the Process

What actually happens on assessment day.

We have heard parents call this "the test." It isn't, really — and it isn't meant to feel like one. Here is exactly what your child will experience, by age, with no surprises.

Pre-school
Nursery · LKG · UKG

This is not a written test. A class teacher and a counsellor sit with your child for a 20–30 minute play-based interaction — colouring, simple puzzles, picking up toys, listening to a short story, and a little chat. We are looking for readiness, curiosity and social comfort, not letters or numbers. Parents are with the child for the first few minutes and just outside the room afterwards. Please do not coach your child or quiz them in advance — it makes them anxious without helping.

Format
20–30 min · Play-based
Parent stays nearby
Primary lateral entry
Class I – V

A short, friendly written interaction in English, Hindi and Mathematics, set at the level of the class your child is applying to. The teacher reads the questions aloud where appropriate. There is also a brief reading-aloud session and a small chat with the class teacher. We are looking for class fit and any catch-up support that may be useful — never to filter a child out who simply needs a settling term.

Format
~45 min written + 10 min chat
Subjects per class level
Middle & Secondary
Class VI – X

A written assessment in English, Hindi, Mathematics and Science (and Social Science from Class VIII), at the level of the class being applied to. This is followed by a 10–15 minute personal interaction with the academic head and the relevant subject teacher. For Class IX and X, the previous year's CBSE / school report card is reviewed alongside.

Format
Written assessment + 10–15 min interaction
Conducted at Senior Branch
Class XI · Stream selection
Post Class X result

For Class XI, there is no separate written test for SPS students continuing from Class X. Stream allocation — Science, Commerce or Humanities — is based on the CBSE Class X result, subject preferences declared at the end of Class X, and the recommendation from the academic counsellor. New students applying from outside also bring their CBSE Class X mark sheet for the same conversation. Yes — every Class XI applicant gets a one-to-one counselling session before locking in a stream.

Format
Counselling-led
Class X result + interview
No child is judged on a single day. We are looking for the right fit — for them and for us.

After Admission

What happens between confirmation and the first morning.

Once your child has a seat, our work really begins. Here is the rhythm of the days between admission and the first morning of school — so nothing is unexpected.

Step 1 · The week of admission

Welcome call & portal set-up

A welcome call from the class teacher within five working days. Parent Portal credentials are issued, the family WhatsApp group for the class is shared, and you are walked through the school's app and notice-board.

Step 2 · 4–6 weeks before session

Orientation & Welcome Day

A half-day on campus where new students meet their class teacher and classmates, walk through the school, and parents receive the year's calendar, syllabus, and key policies.

Step 3 · 3–4 weeks before

Uniform, books & supplies

Uniform details and the CBSE booklist are provided at orientation. A small starter stationery kit is distributed on Day 1.

Step 4 · 2–3 weeks before

Transport registration

The SPS school bus fleet is GPS-tracked and runs on fixed routes across Almora and surrounding areas. Route map and pickup-point allocation is confirmed with the transport office on registration.

Step 5 · Ongoing

Parent communication channels

Daily: school diary (Nursery–V) or Parent Portal app (VI–XII). Weekly: class WhatsApp group moderated by the class teacher. Monthly: digital progress note. Termly: formal Parent–Teacher Meeting.

Step 6 · Day 1

The first morning

You will be expected at the gate by 8:00 AM. The class teacher is at the door. Parents of Nursery–KG students are welcome to stay for the first 30 minutes; parents of older students are walked through a short coffee briefing in the visitor lounge.

Day 1 Checklist

What to bring on the first morning.

Keep it light — we'd rather your child arrive at 8 a.m. unhurried, than fully equipped. Anything missing on Day 1 is genuinely fine; we'll sort it together that week.

  • School ID card & transport tag (issued at orientation)
  • School diary with the parent-acknowledgement page signed
  • Water bottle (refillable) — labelled with the child's name
  • Nutritious snack & lunch box (cafeteria opt-in if registered)
  • Stationery as per the orientation list — no extra books needed Day 1
  • One change of clothes for Nursery–KG, in the school bag
  • An emergency contact card with two numbers, in the front pocket of the bag

We are here every step of the way. Sharda Public School is a community first, and an institution second — and from your child's first morning, you are part of it.

Admissions FAQ

The questions parents ask most often.

If your question is not here, the admissions office picks up the phone every Mon–Sat between 8:30 AM and 2:30 PM at +91 73005 02640.

What is the last date to apply for Session 2025–26?

Admissions for each session run from April to September, and forms are accepted on a rolling basis until classes fill. We strongly recommend applying early; popular classes fill first.

Can my child join mid-year — after April?

Yes, in Classes I to IX, against any vacancy that may exist. Mid-year admissions follow the same process — enquiry, registration, a short academic interaction and confirmation. You will additionally need the current session's progress reports from the previous school, and a migration certificate if transferring across boards. Class X and XII mid-year admissions are accepted only in exceptional circumstances per CBSE rules.

Is there a written entrance test for Nursery, LKG or UKG?

No. The pre-school assessment is a 20–30 minute play-based interaction — not a test. We are looking for readiness, curiosity and social comfort, never academic skills. Please do not coach your child or quiz them in advance; it makes them anxious without helping.

My child studied in a Hindi-medium school. Can they join SPS?

Yes — and many of our current students started exactly that way. SPS is an English-medium school, but our middle and primary teachers are trained to support a transition. Where helpful, we suggest a four-to-six-week language-bridge programme using our remedial track, included in the regular fee. The aim is for your child to feel settled by the end of the first term.

What is the medium of instruction at SPS?

English is the medium of instruction across all subjects, with Hindi as a compulsory second language up to Class X. In Nursery to Class II classrooms are deliberately bilingual — children move between English and Hindi through the day, with Hindi storytelling protected as a daily practice. Sanskrit, French and Kumaoni Studies are offered as third-language electives in middle school.

Are there seats reserved for EWS / weaker sections?

The RTE Act, 2009 mandates 25% reservation in entry-level classes of unaided private schools for children from economically weaker sections and disadvantaged groups. The admissions office shares the application window and documents required. We are committed to supporting these admissions wholeheartedly.

Does SPS offer school transport? Which areas are covered?

Yes. The SPS school bus fleet is GPS-tracked, with female attendants on each bus and certified drivers. Routes cover central Almora, Khatyari, Dwarahat road, Dugalkhola, Mall Road and surrounding pickup points. Pickup-point allocation is confirmed at the time of registration with the transport office.

Can I get the fee structure online or over the phone?

The full fee structure is shared in person at the admissions office or by email on request, because optional add-ons (transport, cafeteria, kits) materially change the total. We are very happy to send you the complete itemised sheet — please email admission@shardapublicschool.com or call us, and we will share it the same day.

My child is appearing for Class X boards. Can I apply for Class XI now?

Yes. Class XI registration opens before CBSE Class X results are announced; you can submit a provisional registration form now and confirm the seat within 15 days of the result. Stream allocation — Science, Commerce or Humanities — happens through a one-to-one counselling session once results are in.

Is there a sibling discount or priority for brothers and sisters of existing students?

As a general rule, families with an existing child at SPS are warmly encouraged to apply early — we want to keep brothers and sisters together where we can.
